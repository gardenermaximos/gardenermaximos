### OVERVIEW

Mechanical Design and Hardware Engineer specializing in **Physical AI, Systems Engineering, and Autonomous Robotics**. Driven by *Mens et Manus*, I bridge mathematical rigor with physical execution—transforming parametric CAD models and control algorithms into low-latency, field-ready hardware.

* **Robotics & Hardware Leadership:** Hardware Lead for a global Top-6 RoboCup team; engineered national championship-winning mobile robot chassis and custom power distribution systems.
* **Prototyping & Digital Manufacturing:** 4+ years in Fusion 360, 300+ modeled components, and 1,000+ manufacturing iterations across FDM, SLA, and Laser Cutting.
* **Open Source & Social Impact:** Founder of STEMPlan and open-source contributor, documenting production-grade CAD blueprints and mentoring young builders in robotics.
