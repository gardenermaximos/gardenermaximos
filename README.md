## /// ABOUT ME

I am a Mechanical Lead and Hardware Engineer driven by building physical systems that interact seamlessly with the real world. Rooted in mechanical design, automation, and control systems, I bring 5+ years of hands-on experience in CAD modeling, physical fabrication, and mobile robotics—focusing on Physical AI and low-latency embedded hardware.

### [ + ] CORE COMPETENCIES & IMPACT
* **Hardware Architecture:** Designed 300+ 3D models and engineered 8+ autonomous soccer robots for RoboCup (Lightweight & Infrared categories).
* **Algorithmic Efficiency:** Researching low-overhead mathematical approximations (Taylor Series variations) to replace heavy calculations on microcontrollers.
* **Open-Source Hardware:** Standardizing and publishing production-ready CAD models and documentation to lower the barrier for robotics teams in Brazil.
* **STEM Mentorship:** Founded **StemPlan**, mentoring 64+ students and providing support to 9+ rookie competitive robotics teams nationwide.
